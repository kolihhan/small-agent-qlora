1. [Verified: `docs/v3d-results.md`] A gain on 200 controlled synthetic policy tasks (v3B 52.5%→v3D 62%) does not guarantee a gain on GAIA (18%→18%).
2. [Verified: `docs/gaia-v3d-results.md`] v3D GAIA finalization regressed 44%→25%; 20 paired final→nonfinal vs 1 reverse, with no decrease in tool errors. Treat termination as a mechanism hypothesis, not proof that a post-hoc stop rule improves correct answers.
3. [Verified: `docs/pro-research-gates.md`] MuSiQue long-form tool use fails despite access to relevant text. In one 8-task cohort only 2/8 visited every annotated supporting path. Reading a literal or formatting an answer differently alone is not a correctness gain.

11. [Verified: Actions 43805908527, 43892091237] Physically successful training does not imply transfer. Native40 8-step QLoRA improved native 2Wiki VALIDATION8 raw strict exact only **1/8→2/8**, while both arms executed the exact same count of working local tools (58), zero tool errors and obtained full annotated support on the same six cases. This does not establish that the adapter improved retrieval or multihop reasoning.
12. [Verified: Actions 43901267208] A **scoring-contract confound** can dwarf model changes: preexisting 2Wiki VAL8 answers often ended with the correct title but included preceding explanation. Gold-blind last-line parser scored previously saved Base replies 1/8→7/8 and native40 replies 2/8→8/8; six rendering-only rescues in each. Because selected on already-observed 8 tasks, these counterfactual scores are **POST-HOC ONLY**. Prioritize error decomposition (tool/evidence → decision → final serialization → scorer) and source-disjoint prospective validation over prompt sweeps/extra SFT steps. No GAIA correctness uplift can be inferred.

14. [Verified: Actions 43906372492] Audit the full paired numerator/denominator before celebrating improvements in completion. On 100 already-seen GAIA cases, 66 failed in both arms; among those, original 57 failed to finalize and 9 finalized wrong, whereas format reminder made 52 unfinalized and 14 finalized wrong. Completion rose from 39 to 44 but all five extra finals were incorrect in aggregate, leaving accuracy 26→26. Future policies need to improve *conditional correctness when finalizing*, not only increase finalization count. The bucket labels cannot by themselves establish whether a model lacks evidence, plans badly or serializes badly.
